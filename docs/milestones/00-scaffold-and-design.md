### 2.1 ADR 0001: the decisions, and what each beat

ADR 0001 is **Proposed** ([`ADR-0001:5`](../../docs/adr/0001-python-core-versioned-json-app.md#L5)) until open item O1 (the holdout start) is settled. 

- **D1. Python computes, JSON carries, the app renders.** One semver `schema_version`, first released by the M0 commit ([`ADR-0001:61-62`](../../docs/adr/0001-python-core-versioned-json-app.md#L61-L62)). The app is a **tolerant reader**: it checks MAJOR and the fields it uses, not the bundled schema; only the producer validates strictly ([`ADR-0001:70-72`](../../docs/adr/0001-python-core-versioned-json-app.md#L70-L72)). *Beat:* the app computing metrics (two implementations drift), one version per file, array-of-objects JSON.
- **D2. Publish a closed set of unitless results, never the series.** Nothing committed, git history included, may let anyone reconstruct a vendor series or a material part of it ([`ADR-0001:85-92`](../../docs/adr/0001-python-core-versioned-json-app.md#L85-L92)); O9 proposes "material part" as a run of 5 or more values within +/-1 vol point, or any level statistic within +/-10%, from the repo plus public data other than the series ([`ADR-0001:94-104`](../../docs/adr/0001-python-core-versioned-json-app.md#L94-L104)). The earlier "no single day is recoverable" promise was **deleted**: reviews B and D each broke it. D2 commits only a **closed set P** ([`ADR-0001:136-179`](../../docs/adr/0001-python-core-versioned-json-app.md#L136-L179)): ratios to the random walk (the one benchmark), DM statistics and p-values, MZ pass/fail flags, Sharpe/PSR/DSR, counts, turnover and the AR(1) half-life, nothing in vol points or prices. A row qualifies only if its numbers are unchanged when the VIX path is rescaled with *public* SPX held fixed, so SPX-tied rows (`vix_implied`, uncalibrated GARCH on the VIX level) stay local (O8; [`ADR-0001:143-152`](../../docs/adr/0001-python-core-versioned-json-app.md#L143-L152)). Real-data prose is limited to seven qualitative statements ([`ADR-0001:194-209`](../../docs/adr/0001-python-core-versioned-json-app.md#L194-L209)). P is released once and only withdrawn (R1; [`ADR-0001:211-219`](../../docs/adr/0001-python-core-versioned-json-app.md#L211-L219)); dated closes are quoted only for named events away from period boundaries, as hygiene (rule Q; [`ADR-0001:221-233`](../../docs/adr/0001-python-core-versioned-json-app.md#L221-L233)). D2 stays **Proposed** ([`ADR-0001:81-83`](../../docs/adr/0001-python-core-versioned-json-app.md#L81-L83)). *Beat:* committing real JSON, per-cell minimums with refresh rules (B), level statistics with a safety proof (D), and scale-tied rows (E) ([`ADR-0001:300-307`](../../docs/adr/0001-python-core-versioned-json-app.md#L300-L307)).
- **D3. Storage** outside the repo; immutable SHA-256-named raw files. *Beat:* overwriting raw files, a database.
- **D4. One protocol for every model.** NYSE calendar from dates alone ([`ADR-0001:403`](../../docs/adr/0001-python-core-versioned-json-app.md#L403)); expanding window, refit every 63 sessions, schedule passed into every run ([`ADR-0001:520`](../../docs/adr/0001-python-core-versioned-json-app.md#L520)); label-availability leak window **h** sessions ([`ADR-0001:521`](../../docs/adr/0001-python-core-versioned-json-app.md#L521)). RMSE and QLIKE are primary; MAE and MAPE are flagged as not consistent for the mean ([`ADR-0001:468-472`](../../docs/adr/0001-python-core-versioned-json-app.md#L468-L472)). DM uses a rectangular variance estimator whose premise is an assumption ([`ADR-0001:591-595`](../../docs/adr/0001-python-core-versioned-json-app.md#L591-L595)). GARCH is fitted on returns, never the VIX level. *Beat:* rolling windows, GARCH on the VIX, "last 24 months", correcting native forecasts to a mean ([`ADR-0001:672`](../../docs/adr/0001-python-core-versioned-json-app.md#L672)), DM with Newey-West ([`ADR-0001:673`](../../docs/adr/0001-python-core-versioned-json-app.md#L673)).
- **D5. Determinism.** Exact `==` pins plus `uv.lock`; seeds `SeedSequence(seed, spawn_key=(key32(model_id), cutoff_yyyymmdd, member))` ([`ADR-0001:646`](../../docs/adr/0001-python-core-versioned-json-app.md#L646)); every schema pattern ends `$(?!\n)` ([`ADR-0001:650`](../../docs/adr/0001-python-core-versioned-json-app.md#L650)); the Ajv test arrives in M1 ([`ADR-0001:649`](../../docs/adr/0001-python-core-versioned-json-app.md#L649)). *Beat:* positional seed keys (they reshuffle when `models.yaml` is reordered), Python's salted `hash()`, a plain `$`.

### 2.2 ADR 0002: the decisions, and what each beat

ADR 0002 is **Proposed** ([`ADR-0002:5-6`](../../docs/adr/0002-compute-and-numeric-stack.md#L5-L6)), measured here on 2026-10-27. Its acceptance condition (the pins landing in `pyproject.toml` and `uv.lock`) is met (`uv lock --check` exited 0 in my run); flipping the status is your call. 

- **CPU-only torch `2.14.1`**, code device-agnostic. *Beat:* **A** CUDA everywhere (gain not worth the cost, no macOS wheel), **B** CUDA on Linux only (CPU-only Linux machines still download CUDA), **C** PyPI with no index (the surprise behind P4), **D** cu126 (P1), cu128 (caps torch at 2.11.0), cu132.
- **Set the thread count explicitly** (P3). **Keep pmdarima**, though on AR(2) series `auto_arima` never returned (2,0,0) ([`ADR-0002:57-61`](../../docs/adr/0002-compute-and-numeric-stack.md#L57-L61)); *beat:* **E** the statsmodels grid alone, kept as the fallback.
- **Exact `==` pins plus `uv.lock`**: one `torch==2.14.1` resolved to five wheel files and two builds trained to different weights ([`ADR-0002:211-217`](../../docs/adr/0002-compute-and-numeric-stack.md#L211-L217)). *Beat:* the version string alone; **F** pandas 2.x.

If you have not written your predictions, go back to [section 1](#1-predict-before-you-look).

**P1. No.** On the cu126 build (kernels for `sm_80` and `sm_90` only), `is_available()` returned `True` and copies and a matmul worked, but `a.cuda() + 1`, `torch.zeros(3, device='cuda')` and the LSTM failed with `CUDA error: no kernel image is available for execution on the device`. The GB10 is `sm_121`; no wheel ships those kernels, but `sm_120` runs on it ([`ADR-0002:182-185`](../../docs/adr/0002-compute-and-numeric-stack.md#L182-L185)). The matmul worked because cuBLAS is NVIDIA's own library, while `x + 1` is a PyTorch kernel ([`ADR-0002:196-200`](../../docs/adr/0002-compute-and-numeric-stack.md#L196-L200)); review A reproduced it ([`Review A:49`](../../docs/validation/M0-review-A-code-numerics.md#L49)). *Lesson:* run a real model, forward and backward.

**P2. About 1.3 times faster on the GPU at 64 units; parity at 32.** 0.114 s per epoch on the GPU against 0.145 s on the CPU with 8 threads at 64 units ([`ADR-0002:136`](../../docs/adr/0002-compute-and-numeric-stack.md#L136)); 0.081 s against 0.079 s at 32 ([`ADR-0002:135`](../../docs/adr/0002-compute-and-numeric-stack.md#L135)). Review A re-measured 0.86 (GPU slower) and 1.26 ([`Review A:50`](../../docs/validation/M0-review-A-code-numerics.md#L50)). For 40 fits, 5 parallel GPU processes took 14.7 s against 33.0 s for 20 CPU processes ([`ADR-0002:146-148`](../../docs/adr/0002-compute-and-numeric-stack.md#L146-L148)): 2.2 times, inside the ADR's "2.0 to 2.3" ([`ADR-0002:150`](../../docs/adr/0002-compute-and-numeric-stack.md#L150)). The GPU is latency-bound at this size.

**P3. Much slower: 6.7 to 20 times** ([`ADR-0002:51-52`](../../docs/adr/0002-compute-and-numeric-stack.md#L51-L52)). 20 threads took 0.754 s per epoch at 32 units against 0.080 s at 8 threads, and 1.144 s against 0.152 s at 64 units ([`ADR-0002:488-501`](../../docs/adr/0002-compute-and-numeric-stack.md#L488-L501)). Review A re-measured 13.0 and 9.1 times ([`Review A:51`](../../docs/validation/M0-review-A-code-numerics.md#L51)). *Consequence:* the LSTM code sets and records the thread count.

**P4. A CUDA build, about 3.2 GB.** PyPI's `torch==2.14.1` on Linux aarch64 reports `2.14.1+cu130`, passed the GPU probe, and pulled in 3,163 MB ([`ADR-0002:283-286`](../../docs/adr/0002-compute-and-numeric-stack.md#L283-L286)), against a 159 MB wheel from the CPU index ([`ADR-0002:452-453`](../../docs/adr/0002-compute-and-numeric-stack.md#L452-L453)). Review A confirmed 3,163.2 MB, of which 2,456.5 MB is NVIDIA wheels ([`Review A:52`](../../docs/validation/M0-review-A-code-numerics.md#L52)).

**P5. 29 to 38 calendar days, mean 30.50** (NYSE, 2000 to 2025; [`ADR-0001:500`](../../docs/adr/0001-python-core-versioned-json-app.md#L500)). Conversely 30 calendar days hold 15 to 22 sessions, mean 20.64 ([`ADR-0001:499`](../../docs/adr/0001-python-core-versioned-json-app.md#L499)), and a year has 251.50 sessions ([`ADR-0001:501`](../../docs/adr/0001-python-core-versioned-json-app.md#L501)); review B re-computed all three ([`Review B:41`](../../docs/validation/M0-review-B-maths-docs.md#L41)). Theory: [concept 01](../concepts/01-forecast-targets-and-annualisation.md), section 2.

**P6. Yes if unanchored; no if anchored.** In a throwaway repo, `data/` made `git check-ignore -v` report `.gitignore:1:data/` for both `app/src/data/term-structure.synthetic.json` and `app/public/data/synthetic/manifest.json`; `/data/` reported nothing. On the real repo plain `git check-ignore --no-index` exits 1 (not ignored) for both files, while `data/raw/x.csv` exits 0 and matches `.gitignore:7:/data/`; with `-v` the first file prints the negating pattern ([`.gitignore:61`](../../.gitignore#L61)), which also means not ignored, and the second prints nothing. The file says so ([`.gitignore:5-6`](../../.gitignore#L5-L6)).

**P7. The VIX path, up to a starting constant.** The one-day error is `e_t = V_{t+1} - V_t`; the sum over `t = 0 .. T-1` telescopes to `V_T - V_0`, and the partial sums give `V_t - V_0` at every date ([`ADR-0001:129`](../../docs/adr/0001-python-core-versioned-json-app.md#L129)). So per-date forecasts, errors, actuals and equity curves count as the licensed data. Theory: [concept 03](../concepts/03-reproducibility-and-data-contracts.md), section 8.

**P8. Something lower: about 18.79.** For a lognormal with median 20 and log-sd 0.25 the mean `20 * exp(0.03125) = 20.635` minimises squared error; the median 20.000 minimises absolute error; the MAPE minimiser is the "median of order -1", `20 * exp(-0.0625) = 18.788`. Measured with 2,000,000 draws: 18.787 ([`ADR-0001:461-466`](../../docs/adr/0001-python-core-versioned-json-app.md#L461-L466); [`tools/probes/mape_check.py:12`](../../tools/probes/mape_check.py#L12)); review B got 18.785 ([`Review B:33`](../../docs/validation/M0-review-B-maths-docs.md#L33)). MAPE of the mean is 0.2118, of the median 0.2037, of 18.788 only 0.1974: a biased-low forecast wins, so ADR 0001 makes RMSE and QLIKE primary and flags MAE and MAPE ([`ADR-0001:468-472`](../../docs/adr/0001-python-core-versioned-json-app.md#L468-L472)).

**P9. Yes, one difference of two closes, and a larger n does not help.** `n * bias = V_end - V_start`, up to the rounding of the published digits. Review B's attack at n = 400 recovered 12.5600 against a true 12.5600 ([`Review B:66`](../../docs/validation/M0-review-B-maths-docs.md#L66)); one quoted close then gives a level, and a refresh reveals `V_E2 - V_E1` the same way ([`Review B:67-68`](../../docs/validation/M0-review-B-maths-docs.md#L67-L68)). Hence the publication set P holds no bias and no level-unit number ([`ADR-0001:181-192`](../../docs/adr/0001-python-core-versioned-json-app.md#L181-L192)), and R1 forbids refreshes ([`ADR-0001:211-219`](../../docs/adr/0001-python-core-versioned-json-app.md#L211-L219)). Second moments leaked the same way (section 7).
