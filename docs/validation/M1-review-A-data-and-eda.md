## New findings (not in the claim list)

- **N1 (medium): the data pipeline writes real data wherever `VOL_DATA_DIR` points, including
  inside the repo.**
  - Run: `VOL_DATA_DIR=<repo copy>/localdata` with `fetch`, `build-interim`, `verify`, `check`
    and `eda`. All exited 0.
  - `git status` then lists these as untracked and **not ignored**:
    `.../eda/<run_id>/eda_report.json` (every EDA number), `.../interim/build_report.json` (first
    and last dates, row counts, raw hashes) and `.../raw/provenance.jsonl` (raw hashes).
  - `.gitignore` covers only `/data/`.
  - The export tool refuses this location; the pipeline does not. The README says the data goes
    "never into the repo", but nothing enforces it.
  - Fix: run `_why_inside_a_checkout` on `config.data_layout().root` in `cli.main`, and ignore
    `*.jsonl` and `eda_report.json`.
- **N2 (low): a real-data byte count in the walkthrough, and real dated closes in test
  fixtures.** See claim 10.
- **N3 (low): the VIX1Y tenor is 366 days in Cboe's methodology.** The project uses 365 and
  calls 9, 93 and 184 unverified, though they are verified.
- **N4 (design, low-medium): M1 computes and prints holdout statistics before any model exists.**
  - `run_eda` prints the holdout half-life. The report holds holdout ADF/KPSS, VRP and contango
    figures.
  - That is a look at the holdout before hyper-parameters are frozen (ADR 4.6 "one look", L6),
    and `holdout_looks` is not incremented.
  - Also, the "dev" EDA sample includes burn-in (1990-2000), while the calendar's `dev` starts at
    2000-03-15. Say which one P's per-period half-life means.
- **N5 (medium): 9 of 11 meaningful mutants survive all 578 tests.** They are V1-V6, V8, V9
  and V11 (claim 9). In particular, nothing tests that:
  - the IV-RV spread's date alignment is right;
  - the 95% level of the half-life CI is right;
  - the HC1 scaling is right;
  - the EDA ignores data past `data_end`.
- **N6 (low):** `LEARNING_LOG.md` still shows M1 as "Planned" and M0 as "Current".
- **N7 (low): durability.** There is no directory `fsync` after `os.link`. A power loss can keep
  the fsynced log line and lose the directory entry, and `verify` would then report the snapshot
  missing.
